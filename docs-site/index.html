Analysis | Visualization | Validation

  • Works with any CF-compliant netCDF data

Visualize simulations

Open anything

A directory, a single file, or a THREDDS/OPeNDAP address.

True-proportion maps

Pick a variable, time and level and see it mapped, zoomed and panned.

Any grid

Regular, curvilinear and unstructured grids, including NEMO, FVCOM, ICON and FESOM.

Depth levels

Step through the vertical, even when the output carries no depth values.

Quick to open

File times are cached and data is never loaded whole, so big output stays responsive.

Learn about the viewer

Analyze simulations

Climatologies

Maps of the annual mean and of each month, at the surface, the bottom or through the water column.

Time series

Long-term and seasonal series for the whole domain, regions or stations.

Phenology

The peak day of the year from daily output.

Changes and transects

Compare periods on maps, and draw surface and vertical sections along any line.

Compare simulations

Put 2–5 runs side by side on one colour scale, on a common grid if you like.

Figure code included

Copy the code behind any figure to reproduce or adapt it.

Validate simulations

Fast to first result

Install from conda-forge and validate a simulation the same day, not after weeks of scripting.

Built-in observation recipes

WOA23, COBE2, OCC-CI, GLODAP and NSBC datasets are ready to use — downloaded and processed for you.

Rigorous statistics

Bias, RMSD, correlation, seasonality, spatial patterns and vertical profiles, computed on matched data.

Publication-quality reports

A shareable HTML report with figures, tables and methods documented automatically, ready to send to collaborators.

Works with your model

NEMO, CMEMS, CMIP-style output, and any CF-compliant NetCDF.

Compare simulations

Build side-by-side comparison reports for multiple runs
